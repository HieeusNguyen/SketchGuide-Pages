SketchGuide Support

Need help with SketchGuide? Use the guidance below for common issues.

Camera Permission

If camera tracing is unavailable, open iOS Settings, find SketchGuide, and enable Camera access. You can continue using lightbox mode without camera access.

Saving Drawings

To save a captured drawing, allow SketchGuide to add images to your photo library when iOS asks for permission.

Restore Purchases

If you bought SketchGuide Pro and do not see Pro features, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are managed by Apple through your Apple ID.

Subscriptions

You can manage or cancel your subscription from iOS Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Contact

For support, please open an issue on the SketchGuide GitHub repository.

Privacy
