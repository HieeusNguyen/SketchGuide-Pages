Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SketchGuide is a drawing practice app that helps users trace sketch references with camera-assisted and lightbox-style tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information We Collect

SketchGuide does not require you to create an account. The app may process or collect the following information:

  • Camera access: Used only to show the live camera preview for tracing. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded by SketchGuide.
  • Photo library access: Used when you choose to save a captured drawing image to your photo library.
  • Purchase information: Subscription purchases and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store.
  • Crash data: Non-personal crash diagnostics may be collected to improve app stability and functionality.
  • App preferences: Settings such as language, opacity, haptics, sound, and kid-safe mode may be stored locally on your device.

Advertising and Tracking

SketchGuide may display ads to non-subscribed users. SketchGuide does not use the Apple App Tracking Transparency framework to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide core app features, save your preferences, process purchases through Apple, improve reliability, and support the drawing experience.

Children's Privacy

SketchGuide is designed to be safe and simple for drawing practice. The app does not require account creation or knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us through the support page.

Third-Party Services

SketchGuide may use Apple services for purchases and system permissions. The app may also use app diagnostics or advertising services to support reliability and free access. These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you delete the app or change the settings. Purchase records are managed by Apple. Crash diagnostics, if collected, are retained only as needed to improve app stability.

Your Choices

  • You can disable camera or photo permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • You can delete SketchGuide from your device to remove local app preferences.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need support, please visit the SketchGuide Support page.
